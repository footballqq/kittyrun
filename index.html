❓ How to Play & Rules
⏱️ How long is each game?
⚡
60s Sprint Challenge (Recommended):
Fixed 1-minute sprint! Dash to grab as many treats as possible. Catching ⏰ Pocket Watch gives +5s extra time!
💖
Classic Endless Survival:
No time limit! You have 3 Hearts. Keep dodging hazards and catching sweets to chase world records!
🎯 Scoring & Combo Mechanics
  • Combo Multiplier: Consecutive catches ramp up combo points up to 2.5x!
  • Milestone Bonuses: Reach 5/10/15/20 combos for massive +50/+150/+300/+500 bonus points! 20 combo grants a Free Shield!
  • ⚠️ Drop Penalty: Dropping any sweet treat immediately RESETS your combo to 0!
  • 🌟 FEVER Time: Catching Rainbow Stars fills energy and triggers 6 seconds of double score with zero hazards!
🕹️ Controls
  • Movement: Drag with mouse/touch, or use ⬅️ ➡️ / A D on keyboard.
  • 💨 Dash: Press Shift, double tap canvas, or click the Dash button for an instant speed burst!
🏪 Boutique & 💾 Save System
🎁
🎁 Mystery Boutique box drops from the sky every few hundred points! Catch it to open the shop & get 25 bonus coins. Pause anytime to save your run and resume later!
